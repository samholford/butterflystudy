Surgical treatments of stage I–II endometriosis

About This Study

  • 🦋 Endometriosis is a long-term condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows outside of it, often causing significant pain. The Butterfly Endometriosis Studies are clinical research trials exploring whether a more extensive surgical technique could lead to better, longer-lasting pain relief for people with stage I–II endometriosis.
  • 🦋 We are inviting about 20 people in Auckland to take part in a research study comparing two surgical techniques: standard excision and complete posterior peritonectomy. This study will help determine whether a larger future study is possible and explore whether we can achieve longer-lasting pain relief for stage I–II endometriosis.

Who Can Join?

  • ✓ Are a person with a uterus 18 – 50 years old.
  • ✓ Are scheduled for a laparoscopy for pelvic pain.
  • ✓ Have not had previous surgical treatment for endometriosis.
  • ✓ Agree to take part in the study.

Who Cannot Join?

  • ✕ Being pregnant.
  • ✕ Previous removal of your uterus or ovaries.
  • ✕ Suspected stage III–IV endometriosis before surgery.

Your journey in the study: A 12-Month Plan

📋

Step 1: Screening

We'll confirm your eligibility and you will sign the consent form.

🏥

Step 2: Surgery

You'll be randomly assigned one of two surgical techniques.

💬

Step 3: Follow-up

Online questionnaires and one extra visit after 12 months.

Quick Facts

Study Type: Randomised Controlled Trial

Participants: ~20 people in NZ

Duration: 12 months

Location: Te Toka Tumai Auckland Hospital & Greenlane Surgical Unit

Cost: No cost to you

Compensation: A koha (gift) is provided after completing the 3, 6 and 12 month surveys.
